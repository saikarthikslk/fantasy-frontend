## Bonuses

- **Playing XI bonus:** +4 for every selected player who is in the announced playing XI.
- **Captain multiplier:** All points earned by C are multiplied by **2×**.
- **Vice-Captain multiplier:** All points earned by VC are multiplied by **1.5×**.

---

## ApnaXI Adjustments

- **Smart XI auto-pick:** If you miss the deadline and we auto-pick a balanced XI for you, your final score is reduced by **10%**.
- **Auto-bench replacement:** When a selected player is not in the playing XI and we swap in a replacement, the replacement's points count at **90%**.

*Values shown are defaults and may be tuned before each season.*
